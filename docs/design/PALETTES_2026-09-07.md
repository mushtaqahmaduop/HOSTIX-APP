## Why none of this was applied

`renderer/tokens.css` does not use a neutral ladder, and that is a decision with
reasoning written into the file rather than an oversight:

- **Dark theme is warm.** `--surface: #1F1E1B`, `--surface-sunken: #181715`,
  `--text-primary: #FAF9F5`. The file records a contrast regression that came
  from putting a neutral grey on the warm ground.
- **Light theme is cool blue.** `--surface-muted: #FAFBFD`,
  `--surface-border: #E4E7EE`, `--text-primary: #152238`.

Swapping either for a pure neutral would flatten a deliberate temperature
difference between the two themes and reverse the 2026-09-03 ruling that the
dark ground goes warm. If the intent is to move the app to neutral surfaces,
that is a design decision to take explicitly — it is not a token substitution,
because the accent ramp (`--accent-600: #2563eb`) was chosen to sit on tinted
grounds, and CLAUDE.md's rebranding rule says the accent ramp is the only thing
that may be overridden.

**Open question for the owner:** are these a reference for the dark-mode work in
`Hostyllo_Dark_Mode_Brief_Specs(1).md`, or a proposal to replace the tinted
ladders? They cannot be both.
